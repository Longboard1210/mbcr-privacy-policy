Privacy Policy

MBCR Ltd – eBay Management & Growth

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

MBCR Ltd operates this application to manage and analyse its own eBay selling activities. MBCR Ltd is responsible for the personal information it processes through this application.

For privacy enquiries or requests, please contact MBCR Ltd through the Contact Seller facility on our eBay seller account.

2. Information accessed

When authorised by the MBCR Ltd eBay account holder, the application accesses information from eBay including:

  • Active listing IDs, titles, SKUs, prices, available quantities and listing details.
  • Listing performance, including impressions, views, click-through rates and sales metrics.
  • Order IDs, dates, fulfilment status, item IDs, line item IDs, SKUs, item titles, quantities and line item costs.

The connector's order CSV exports are designed to exclude buyer names, email addresses and postal addresses.

3. Why we use this information

We use this information to manage MBCR Ltd's own eBay catalogue, review stock, understand listing performance, analyse sales and improve our product listings.

MBCR Ltd's intended lawful basis for this business activity is legitimate interests in operating and managing its own business. This basis must be assessed and documented by MBCR Ltd before it is relied upon for personal data processing.

4. Where information is stored

The MBCR eBay connector runs locally on an authorised Windows computer. Listing, traffic and order reports are saved as CSV files on that computer. Application credentials and OAuth refresh tokens are also stored locally in configuration files.

The connector does not automatically send these local reports to ChatGPT or another analysis service. MBCR Ltd may choose to share selected exports with an analysis service, such as ChatGPT, for further review.

5. Service providers and sharing

The application communicates with eBay APIs to retrieve authorised account information. Cloudflare hosts the public notification endpoint and privacy page. Cloudflare may process technical request information needed to operate and secure that service.

Where MBCR Ltd voluntarily uploads reports to ChatGPT, those files are processed by OpenAI under the applicable service terms and privacy information.

MBCR Ltd does not sell eBay account information.

6. Retention and security

Local reports are retained only for as long as they are needed for business analysis and record keeping. Information required to meet legal or accounting obligations may be retained for the applicable statutory period.

Access to the connector and its local credentials should be restricted to authorised users. Credentials and tokens must not be shared publicly.

7. Account deletion requests

MBCR Ltd has configured an endpoint to receive eBay Marketplace Account Deletion notifications. The current connector is designed not to export buyer names, email addresses or postal addresses. MBCR Ltd will review valid deletion requests and erase relevant personal information held in its systems unless retention is required by law.

Notification signature verification and automated deletion processing are not yet implemented. These functions must be completed before the notification service is treated as fully operational.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights to request access to personal information, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, objection or data portability.

To make a privacy enquiry or request, contact MBCR Ltd through the Contact Seller facility on our eBay seller account.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application or its data handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this address.
